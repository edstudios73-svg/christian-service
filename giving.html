Easiest Way

Give by Mobile Money

Most of our members give by MoMo. It takes less than a minute.

Steps to give by MoMo

  1. Dial your Mobile Money short code (MTN, Telecel or AirtelTigo).
  2. Choose Pay Bill / Merchant Payment.
  3. Enter the church merchant number.
  4. Enter the amount and put your name plus what it is for, for example "Kofi – Tithe".
  5. Confirm with your PIN and keep the message that comes.

Call or WhatsApp the church office on 0551172156 for the current MoMo merchant number and the church bank account details.
