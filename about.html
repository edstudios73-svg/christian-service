Who We Are

A Bible-believing church family in East Legon, Accra. We worship God, teach His Word and care for people.

Our Identity

Christian Service Church

We are a Bible-believing church on Jungle Avenue Road, East Legon, Accra. Our work is simple: help people know Jesus Christ, grow in Him, and serve others.

Christian Service Church, also known as Christian Service Centre, was started by the late Rev. Captain (Rtd) James C. Ocran. Today the church is led by his son, Rev. Dr. Joseph Payin Ezekiel, who serves as General Overseer. We are also called the House of Testimonies, because we believe God still answers prayer and people still have stories to tell. The church is not the building. The church is the people. That is why we look after one another - in good times and in hard times.

Our main service is every Sunday at 9:00 AM. During the week we hold prayer meeting, Bible study, women's meetings and youth meetings. Whether you are new to the faith or you have followed Christ for many years, there is a place for you here. You can plan your visit or call us on 0551172156.

Our Mission

Mission, Aims & Statement of Faith

From our church in East Legon, Accra, we give glory to God, make disciples, serve our community and share the good news of Jesus Christ.

Our Mission & Aims

To worship God with all our heart, teach the Bible well, train members to serve, build a loving church family, and carry the love of Christ from East Legon to other places.

Statement of Faith

We believe the Bible is the Word of God. We believe in God the Father, God the Son and God the Holy Spirit. We believe Jesus Christ is Lord, that salvation is by grace through faith in Him, and that every believer should live a holy life and serve others.

Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To see lives changed by the gospel, believers growing to be like Christ, and every member finding and doing the work God has given them - in Accra, in Ghana and beyond.

Our Mission

To bring people closer to Jesus Christ by worshipping God, making disciples, serving with love, and sharing the gospel with everybody we meet.

"We are here to glorify God by making true followers of Jesus Christ - people who love Him, live like Him, and lead others to Him."
