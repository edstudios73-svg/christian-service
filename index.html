What We Believe

A Church Built on
The Word and The Spirit

Everything we do comes from the Bible and is led by the Holy Spirit.

Worship

We gather to praise the name of Jesus through singing, prayer and the preaching of God's Word.

Bible Teaching

We teach the Bible plainly so that every member can grow strong in faith and live it at home and at work.

Family

We are one family. We carry one another's burdens, check on one another and help one another.

Prayer

We believe prayer works. We pray together every Monday at 6:30 PM and stand with members in every situation.
