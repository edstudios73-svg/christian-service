Privacy Policy

Caring for the information entrusted to our church family.

A Trust We Honour

Your information deserves care

Last updated: September 19, 2026

1. Who we are

Christian Service Church, located at Jungle Avenue Road, East Legon, Accra, Ghana, operates this website and its online ministry forms. This policy explains what information we receive, why we use it, and the choices available to you.

2. Information you choose to share

Depending on how you use the website, you may choose to provide your name, phone number, email address, planned visit details, prayer request, testimony, event registration, or message to the church. Please share only what is needed for us to respond and support you.

3. How we use information

We use submitted information to respond to requests, prepare for visits, organize church activities, moderate public submissions, communicate important announcements, improve the website, and provide pastoral follow-up when appropriate. We do not sell personal information or use prayer requests for advertising.

4. Prayer requests and sensitive information

Prayer requests may contain sensitive personal details. They are handled for ministry and pastoral purposes by authorized church workers. Please avoid sharing information that is not necessary. This website is not an emergency service; contact the appropriate emergency or professional support service when immediate help is needed.

5. Announcements and notifications

If you choose to enable browser notifications, your device may receive new church announcements. You can turn notifications off at any time in your browser or device settings. The website may store a local read status on your device so announcement badges work properly.

6. Cookies, storage, and analytics

The website may use browser storage for preferences such as installation prompts, notification status, and announcement read status. We may use privacy-conscious website analytics to understand general visits and improve the site. We do not use these tools to sell personal profiles or read private content from your device.

7. Service providers and protection

We use trusted technical services for hosting, secure forms, content storage, authentication, notifications, maps, and website operation. Access to submitted information is limited to people and services that need it for those purposes. We use reasonable safeguards, but no internet transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Retention and your choices

We keep information only as long as it is reasonably needed for ministry, administration, legal, security, or record-keeping purposes. You may ask what personal information we hold, request a correction, or ask us to remove information where appropriate. Some records may need to be retained for accountability or legal reasons.

9. Children and changes

Parents and guardians should guide children when they use online forms. We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children. We may update this policy as the website develops; the date above shows when it was last reviewed.

For privacy questions or requests, email info@christianservicechurch.org or use our contact page.
